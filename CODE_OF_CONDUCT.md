# 行為準則 / Code of Conduct

## English

We want this project to be a friendly and respectful learning community, open to people of every background and level of experience.

**Expected behavior**

- Respect different viewpoints and experiences, and stay focused on the topic.
- When pointing out a mistake, explain the problem clearly and give your sources where possible.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone starts out not knowing.
- Accept constructive criticism, and focus on making the content better.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Personal attacks, insults, discrimination or harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Posting confidential information of any company, or asking others to provide it.
- Advertising, spam or content unrelated to the project.

**Enforcement**

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who violate it. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please use GitHub's "Report content" feature or contact the maintainers.

This code of conduct is written in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

---

## 繁體中文

我們希望這個專案是一個友善、互相尊重的學習社群，歡迎不同背景、不同程度的人一起參與。

**我們期待的行為**

- 尊重不同的觀點與經驗，就事論事。
- 指正錯誤時，具體說明問題，並盡量附上依據。
- 對新手保持耐心，每個人都是從不懂開始的。
- 接受建設性的批評，把注意力放在讓內容變得更好。

**不被接受的行為**

- 人身攻擊、侮辱、歧視或騷擾。
- 未經同意公開他人的私人資訊。
- 張貼任何公司的機密資訊，或要求他人提供機密資訊。
- 廣告、垃圾訊息，或與專案無關的內容。

**處理方式**

維護者有權刪除、編輯或拒絕不符合本準則的留言、Issue 與 Pull Request，必要時可以暫時或永久禁止違規者參與本專案。如果你遇到或看到不當的行為，可以使用 GitHub 的檢舉功能（Report content），或直接聯絡專案維護者。

本準則參考 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 的精神撰寫。
